# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Barcode Benchmark welcoming and respectful
for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- State benchmark limitations honestly and make results reproducible.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and the provenance of submitted images and datasets.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Submitting confidential images, payloads, credentials, or customer data.
- Manipulating, fabricating, or intentionally misrepresenting benchmark results.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the project in
public, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and community events.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that
violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through
[HammadAshrafDev's GitHub profile](https://github.com/HammadAshrafDev). Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible. A maintainer involved in a report
must not take part in deciding its outcome.

## Enforcement guidelines

Responses should be proportionate to the impact and pattern of behavior:

1. **Correction:** A private explanation and request to correct minor inappropriate
   behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning for a more serious or repeated violation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A time-limited restriction from project interaction.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Removal from future project participation for sustained,
   severe, or dangerous behavior.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for this project's size
and benchmarking/data-handling risks.
